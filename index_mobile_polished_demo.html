How Escrow Works

A clear four-step process for a more organized transaction.

1

Seller Places Order

The transaction details and agreed terms are recorded before the workflow begins.

2

Buyer Uploads Verification

The buyer provides the required order confirmation or verification information.

3

System Verification

The submitted information is reviewed against the transaction requirements.

4

Payment Release

Once the agreed conditions are satisfied, the transaction proceeds to its release stage.
