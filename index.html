Trade Rules & Guidelines
Buyer Protected
Please read and follow these rules carefully.
1
Payment is 100% Secure

Buyer’s payment is fully secured in escrow and will be released after successful verification.

2
Cancellation Request

If you want to cancel the trade, you must provide full chat records (complete verification process) explaining the reason. If the buyer does not agree, you still need to provide a chat record.

3
Be Honest and Cooperative

Provide valid information and cooperate with the buyer. Any misleading information may result in account restrictions.

4
Respect the Buyer

Buyer has higher priority in our system. Always communicate politely and respond to the buyer’s requests in a timely manner.

5
Follow Platform Instructions

Complete all required steps and upload correct documents. This helps ensure a smooth and fast verification process.

Buyer’s Payment Will Be Released

Once the verification is completed successfully, the payment will be released to the buyer 100%.
